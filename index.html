Specific Blockages We Fix

Every drain behaves differently. We bring specialized manual tools to address the unique cause of each local plumbing blockage.

Kitchen Sinks

Food remnants, cooking oil, and grease create rock-hard blockages in trap loops. We clear grease traps and unblock the pipes to restore free flow.

Blocked Toilets

Toilet backups can create unhygienic conditions quickly. We use heavy toilet augers to clear toilet paper and foreign objects without scratching the porcelain.

Showers & Tubs

Hair bundles tangled with soap scum settle deep inside drain traps, slowing drainage. We pull these blocks out manually to prevent recurring pooling.

Bathroom Sinks

Often clogged by toothpaste residue, beard hair, and soap. We disassemble or clear the pop-up stopper and clean the lines thoroughly.

Main Sewer Line

When multiple drains backup simultaneously, the root cause is in the main line. We use professional rodding rods to break up severe clogs deep underground.

Drain Rodding

Heavy-duty manual rod sets are pushed into sewer pipelines to break up solid grease and roots, clearing out lines where typical store-bought snakes fail.

Our Drain Unblocking Process

We work systematically to ensure your drainage issue is completely resolved and doesn't return immediately after we leave.
